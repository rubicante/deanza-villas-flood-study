  - Notes: County guidance describing alluvial fan flood protection requirements and the Borrego Zone AO / depth-velocity context.

## Key extracted context

- Boyle/County guidance: all areas on the fan are subject to flooding unless appropriate flood protection is provided.
- DRI: about 90% of the 61 sq mi study area is geomorphically and hydraulically active alluvial fan landforms.
- FEMA/County guidance: alluvial fan hazards in Borrego Springs are mapped as Zone AO with depth/velocity information.
